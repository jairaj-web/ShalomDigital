Our story

Founded on a Simple Promise

ShalomDigital was founded in Bengaluru after our founders watched too many small businesses burn money on marketing agencies that delivered jargon instead of results.

We started with one principle: treat every client's budget like our own. That promise turned a two-person side project into a full-service agency trusted by growing businesses across Bengaluru.

Today we're a team of strategists, designers, writers and data nerds — still guided by the same idea: marketing should bring you peace of mind, not headaches.
